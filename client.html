What works now

Browsing: real CSS, images, fonts and scripts; links, forms, Back/Forward; a tab strip with ctrl/cmd-click, + and ×; an address bar that takes bare domains and searches.

Redirects: rewritten rather than swallowed, so the address bar and history update the way a browser does, and links that ask for a new window open a new proxied tab.

Sessions: the relay keeps a domain- and path-aware cookie jar, so logins, carts and preferences survive — including multi-host logins like auth.example.com.

Media: opening a video, audio or stream URL through the relay gives you a real player page — it plays there instead of downloading. Byte ranges are proxied so seeking works, HLS (m3u8) is played natively where the browser supports it and via hls.js otherwise, DASH via dash.js, and every segment stays inside the relay. Play media ↗ opens that player for whatever is in the box.

Still out: bot-walled sites (Google, Cloudflare challenges, banks) refuse datacenter IPs; WebSockets need the Workers/Deno build or the Node adapter; service workers are blocked by design.
